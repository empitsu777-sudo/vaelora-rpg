# Vaelora — Canon and Decision Register
Version: 0.1 | Established: 2026-10-15 | Status: foundation, not final story approval

## User-established world rules (retain)
- **Matter is frozen music.** The First Chorus shaped the world through resonance. Kith are living melodies that willingly wear physical shells assembled from their environment.
- **Six affinities:** Verdant, Relic, Zephyr, Umbra, Abyssal and Cinder.
- **Dominance:** Verdant > Relic > Zephyr > Umbra > Abyssal > Cinder > Verdant.
- **Chord reactions:** Over-Pitch (Cinder + Zephyr; firestorm/updraft), Galvanic Lock (Abyssal + Relic; wet conductive metal/kinetic lock), Parasitic Bloom (Verdant + Umbra; thorn life transfer), Vitrification (Cinder + Relic with rapid cooling; brittle glass then physical shatter).
- **Lantern:** exactly three active Glass Plates; other plates in Glyph Satchel are dormant. Change active Triad at Chime Hearths. Heavy environmental abilities spend Liquid Echo; refill through rhythm, pots, water-chimes.
- **Decoupling:** destroyed Kith shells collapse without killing the spirit. Violent decoupling cracks a Plate (Dissonance); repair using Resonant Amber or a Hearth. All three plates cracked triggers rescue to last Hearth with some ordinary material loss.
- **Shell-Grafting:** shell properties depend on regional material; reshaping occurs at Hearths.
- **Crucible Awakening:** eight-note/full-octave bond, appropriate regional catalyst, landmark survival rite.
- **Society:** Bastion Spires protect settlements from the Hush; glaziers make plates and lenses.
- **Ethics:** binding is voluntary. Brass Synod uses Dead-Lead, Null-Chains and anti-chords to trap Kith in Amalgams; gameplay must prioritize freeing them, never simply destroying trapped spirits.

## Approved directional change
**Vaelora is an original top-down action-adventure RPG**, not a Pokémon-style monster-collecting RPG. Core pillars:
1. Discover a living six-region world and distinct settlements.
2. Customize a Wayfarer avatar, find meaningful loot, equipment and materials, and craft.
3. Meet NPCs, accept quests, earn trust and change local conditions.
4. Fight and solve terrain puzzles alongside a *consenting* Kith companion.
5. Use acoustic/chemical Chords in real-time exploration and combat.
6. Render attractive, legible, handcrafted pixel-art environments on native 240x160 GBA hardware.

## Current design proposals; not locked canon
- One visible active Kith companion with rapid assists from the other two Plates, to fit GBA performance.
- Real-time action combat with dodge/guard, player weapons and reactive terrain.
- Custom avatar identity/cosmetics; Liora Vale becomes a potential mentor/default template, not necessarily fixed protagonist.
- Mostly handmade rewards rather than endless randomized loot.
- Story seed: Bellhaven's Spire begins erasing memories ('The Bell That Forgot a Child'); Sol Veyr argues safety requires compulsory synchronization.
- Six regions, eighteen named settlements, six Sanctums: **scope target**, not promise of already implemented content.

## Unresolved design tensions
1. Distinguish beneficial silence/rest from the coercive Hush.
2. Show and respect Kith consent in bonding and story choices.
3. Balance cracked plates and evacuation to prevent soft locks.
4. Give Null-Chains limits and meaningful counters.
5. Respect physical constraints: rust requires susceptible conductive metal; vitrification requires credible hot/cold sequence.
6. Every mandatory Chord puzzle must be solvable with nearby Hearth access and clear cues.
7. Story branching should be meaningful but sustainable for a GBA cartridge.
