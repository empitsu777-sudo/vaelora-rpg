# Sapphire Coast — Town briefs (proposed)

**Tidemark Quay:** Crane masts, whitewashed tide houses and dry dock. Captain Sen Fen runs shipping and connects to Nemi Fen of Reedwake. Fish hall, dock office, beacon. Q_SC1 Names on the Manifest: reconstruct a missing skiff route from tide and cargo evidence. Reward: skiff pass. Return state: repaired beacon and safe dock notices.

**Corallume:** Coral kilns, shell mosaics, reef nursery. Sculptor Luma Tide trades with Aya Root. Atelier and dye workshop; Listening Reef landmark. Q_SC2 A Statue That Listens: investigate an unusual echo-stone monument and redesign the memorial. Reward: coral shell-graft material. Return state: a visibly different sculpture.

**Moonpool Haven:** Moonstone domes and concentric pools. Sister Arune maintains a memory archive and healing spring; landmark Returning Pool. Q_SC3 The Private Tide: reconstruct a storm warning from physical tide records. Reward: Abyssal route resonance. Return state: improved channel markers.

**Returning Tide Sanctum:** An accessible Hearth precedes a Galvanic Lock gate (Abyssal plus Relic on wet conductive metal). Completion opens a safe ore inlet to Ferrum Hollow. Palette: indigo water, rose coral and chalk docks; sound: shell horns and water bells. The optional estuary route to Rootwhisper opens only after the Deepworks chapter.
