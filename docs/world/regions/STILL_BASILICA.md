# Still Basilica — Region 6 (proposed)
**Route:** Quietwatch → Chainfall → Basilica City → Unheard Crown Sanctum. Muted bells, courtyards and Synod halls. Archives and precision relays support pilgrim services; food and metal are imported. Sound: long rests punctuated by clear notes. Chosen quiet is restorative; the Hush is imposed muting.

**Quietwatch:** Low arcades, empty bell frames, watchtower reached through a wind pass. Keeper Noa exchanges messages with Sister Arune, Leth Mirren and Eris Gale. Watch office, sanctuary, Bellless Watch landmark. **Q_SB1 A Rest Is Not a Chain:** investigate altered watch logs and improve sanctuary notices. Reward: Hush-safe lens. Return state: revised signs.

**Chainfall:** Broken restraint pylons, hanging chain bridges and salvage yards. Canon-Marshal Iseult Thren struggles to replace outdated transport methods; Tovan Rusk helped engineer them. Rescue depot, salvage hall, Fallen Link landmark. **Q_SB2 Unfasten the Living:** disable Null-Chains on a stranded Amalgam convoy, protect civilians and escort freed Kith to a Hearth. Reward: safe-release tool. Return state: chain racks repurposed as rescue scaffolds.

**Basilica City:** Crown dome, clapperless avenues and brass inlays; arrival boulevard frames the Unheard Crown. Inquisitor Sol Veyr argues compulsory synchronization is necessary against the Hush. Tribunal, relay vault, artisan quarter. **Q_SB3 The City That Listens:** expose coercive relays and establish voluntary safeguards with local communities. Reward: civic covenant. Return state: distinct bells ring in independent voices.

**Unheard Crown Sanctum:** Hearths allow legal three-Plate swaps through multi-stage puzzles. The finale must free Kith and keep an effective voluntary safety network. Completion reopens the old pilgrim road to Bellhaven. Art: sheltered quiet and imposed muting must be visually distinguishable.
