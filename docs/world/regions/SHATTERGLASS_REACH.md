# Region 1 — Shatterglass Reach (proposed v0.1)
**Map:** Bellhaven → Reedwake → Prismere → Broken Bell Sanctum. **Colors:** limestone cream, amber brass, teal glass. **Music:** uneven bell fifths over dry reeds. **Landmark:** Split Crown Ridge. **Economy:** glass lenses and bell fittings exported to the coast; iron and salt imported. **Custom:** witnessed promises are rung on handbells, but witnesses may correct the record. **Conflict:** Spires protect citizens while damaged archives suppress inconvenient memories.

## Bellhaven (SH1)
**Silhouette / entry:** forked brass Spire above slate roofs; river bridge frames the cracked lower prong. **Culture:** written witness-notes accompany promises. **NPC:** Maera Vell, glazier, conceals that her lens research enabled Synod Null-Chains; former collaborator of Prismere's Iva Cress and Railspire's Tovan Rusk. **Services / interiors:** lens bench, Hearth repairs, glass shop, inn, relay cellar. **Interactive landmark:** Founder's Chime Spire, whose lowest note is absent. **Quest Q_SH1 — The Note That Went Missing:** compare three physical witness clues, restore the market relay, free a trapped Kith. Choose immediate public disclosure or guarded repair-first disclosure; either preserves evidence and consent. **Return state:** honest repair notices replace festival propaganda. **Reward:** Lantern calibration. **Art:** amber glass signs, brass lanterns, distinct bridge arches; no generic village recolor.

## Reedwake (SH2)
**Silhouette / entry:** stilt homes, reed windmills, slanted ferry roofs; a water-chime footpath bends through cattails. **Economy:** mats, freshwater fish, marsh passage. **Custom:** debts paid and forgiven are tied in reed cords. **NPC:** Nemi Fen, ferry warden, sibling of Captain Sen Fen at Tidemark Quay. **Services / interiors:** ferry shed, reed lodge, healer. **Landmark:** Nine-Reed Floodgate; hollow water chimes. **Quest Q_SH2 — A Ferry for the Forgotten:** retune sluice bells and clear a channel without destroying nesting habitat. Choose rapid freight or a slower wildlife-safe ferry; both are usable and alter boat schedules. **Return state:** ferry resumes and birds return. **Reward:** waterproof reed thread. **Shortcut:** Reedwake–Tidemark ferry unlocks after Broken Bell and ferry repair. **Art:** broad reed fans, blue-green marsh stilts.

## Prismere (SH3)
**Silhouette / entry:** sawtooth glass furnaces and sun-catcher towers above glass-sand terraces. **Economy:** Glass Plates, lenses and kiln sand. **Custom:** apprentices mark each piece to own defects. **NPC:** Iva Cress, master glassworker, distrusts Maera's undocumented prototypes and sells cooling glass to Ferrum Hollow. **Services / interiors:** plate-grafting studio, kiln, guild archive, market. **Landmark:** Prism Stair, throwing hard-edged colored caustics. **Quest Q_SH3 — The Furnace Register:** trace kiln stamps to a concealed order for restraint glass; choose workers' evidence council or formal inquiry, neither erases evidence. **Return state:** every kiln shift displays maker marks. **Reward:** optional cosmetic Lantern casing. **Sanctum:** Broken Bell is approached through a glass fissure; tune witness chimes and release the first Amalgam Kith, never attack its spirit. **Art:** faceted rooftops, light shafts, visible kiln chimneys.

**Region pacing:** Bellhaven teaches dialogue, Lantern, voluntary Kith assistance; Reedwake teaches water-chimes and environment-first navigation; Prismere introduces Hearth Plate swaps, provenance clues and the first nonlethal rescue. Accessible nearby Hearth and non-combat fallback for each mandatory puzzle. Broken Bell completion opens the salt-glass escarpment to Tidemark Quay.
