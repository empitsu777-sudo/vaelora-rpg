# Iron-Weep Deepworks — Region 3 (proposed)

Ferrum Hollow, Railspire Junction, Anvilrest; Buried Magnet Sanctum. Oxide red and slate architecture, rail trestles, iron gantries and cooling canals. Exports: magnetite and forged tools; imports: food, timber and cooling water. Residents record safe work shifts. The central conflict is replacing coerced Kith labor without abandoning workers.

**Ferrum Hollow:** Orrin Vale, mine safety steward. Q_IW1 The Third Shift: find unsafe hidden machinery, stabilize tunnels, and organize fair replacement shifts. Landmark: Weeping Ore Face; interiors: union hall and mine office; return: staffed safety station.

**Railspire Junction:** Tovan Rusk, former Synod engineer. Q_IW2 The Unsent Stop Signal: reconstruct a railway timing failure and install a human-witness safety relay. Landmark: Four-Track Dial; interiors: dispatch room and signal workshop; return: redundant stop bells.

**Anvilrest:** Bera Flint, forge forewoman. Q_IW3 Water for the Forge: restore cooling water and protect households during industrial repairs. Landmark: Cooling Wheel; interiors: forge and cooling station; return: redesigned sluices.

**Sanctum:** Buried Magnet. Cinder plus Relic heats a brittle glass barrier, and a fixed sluice cools it before shattering. Rescue trapped Kith before dismantling the old power source. Completion opens the Rust-Root boundary toward Rootwhisper.
