# Vaelora world atlas — 17 October 2026

Status: **PROPOSED**; this world design is not approved canon, implemented maps, or a ROM.

Preserve `docs/CANON.md` and `docs/WORLD_INDEX.md`: six regions, exactly eighteen existing named settlements, six Sanctums, and a target of 24 connecting routes. All Kith bonds must be voluntary; Amalgams must be rescued, not destroyed.

Design strategy: authored region hubs, readable arrival landmarks, distinctive settlement architecture, local trade chains, optional loops, and persistent post-quest changes. New travel gates must never require more than three active Glass Plates; place an accessible Chime Hearth before mandatory reaction puzzles. A "rest" is voluntary silence; the Hush is imposed muting.

Research: Techland's quest/level-design discussion (https://techland.net/blog/places-and-their-stories-quest-design-level-design) emphasizes that places must visibly communicate function; Nintendo's GDC 2017 systems discussion (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/video-designing-i-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-i-s-unconventional-mechanics) motivates discoverable, consistent environmental interactions. Vaelora applies these lessons at GBA scope rather than imitating those games.

Planned outputs: 18 quest-bearing town briefs, 24-edge graph, six Sanctum gates, automated connectivity tests, an explicit art handoff. No maps or art are represented as complete.
