# Vaelora — Six-Region World and Settlement Index
Status: **proposed map plan**, subject to narrative / travel-graph review.
Designed scope: 18 towns, 24 connecting routes, 6 Sanctums; initial interior planning envelope is ~108 settlement rooms plus ~42 Sanctum rooms (not built).

| Region | Identity | Three settlements | Sanctum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shatterglass Reach | Limestone, brass, glass sand, cracked bells | Bellhaven; Reedwake; Prismere | The Broken Bell |
| Sapphire Coast | Coral, ports, reefs, tidal memory | Tidemark Quay; Corallume; Moonpool Haven | The Returning Tide |
| Iron-Weep Deepworks | Magnetite, rail, forges, coerced labor | Ferrum Hollow; Railspire Junction; Anvilrest | The Buried Magnet |
| Thorn Choir | Giant trees, shadowbloom, living bridges | Rootwhisper; Bloomshade; Mourning Grove | The Night Bloom |
| Aerie of Seven Winds | Updrafts, suspended bridges, wind-carved cliffs | Kestrel Roost; Skybraid; Galeheart | The Open Sky |
| Still Basilica | Muted bells, contested sanctuaries, Synod capital | Quietwatch; Chainfall; Basilica City | The Unheard Crown |

## Town production rules
Every settlement must include: a distinct skyline/silhouette; a readable arrival route; local economy and inhabitants; at least one memorable NPC; shops/services aligned to culture; an interactive landmark; at least one meaningful quest and a return-state transformation; interiors with actual purpose; route exits and consistent travel geography; an acoustic identity and unique tileset accents. No simple palette swaps masquerading as towns.

## Region 1 minimum complete vertical slice
Bellhaven (intro Spire town) -> Reedwake (reed marsh and water-chime traversal) -> Prismere (glassmaking center) -> Broken Bell Sanctum; connected wilderness routes, interiors, living NPCs, Chord puzzles, companion combat, loot/equipment, save/load and nonlethal Amalgam rescue.

## Expansion discipline
World content may be added in staged region packs, but never substitute procedural filler for readable, authored spaces. Track actual delivered map data separately from the proposed 18-town scope.
