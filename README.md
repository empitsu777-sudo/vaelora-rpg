# Vaelora: The Last Listening

**Original GBA action-adventure RPG — preproduction**  
*World premise: matter is frozen music. Explore six regions as a customizable Wayfarer, meet independent Kith companions, fight in real time, solve harmonic puzzles, uncover forgotten history, and build a living world through choices.*

> **Current status (2026-10-15):** documentation foundation established, **not a playable production game**. Vaelora v0.1/v0.2 prototypes are superseded as quality targets.

## October 16–18: research and design

1. **Oct 16:** history, factions, First Chorus, Seventh Interval, Hush and Synod.
2. **Oct 17:** towns and regional cultures, NPC webs, quests and travel-network coherence.
3. **Oct 18:** story arc, real-time combat, companion interactions, loot, avatar customization, economy and contradiction audit.

Design direction: **adventure RPG, not Pokémon-style monster collection**. Pokémon Emerald source/ROM were studied for GBA engineering benchmarks only; no Pokémon content belongs in this repository.

## Working rules

Write proposals separately from approved canon. Preserve source references for research. Build and playtest a truly legible 240x160 gameplay vertical slice before mass-producing art or mapping all eighteen towns. Every milestone needs reproducible evidence, not just a successful compiler exit.
